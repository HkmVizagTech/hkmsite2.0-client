# Odia glossary — harekrishnavizag.org

Style: warm, respectful, simple everyday Odia (not heavy literary). Address the
reader respectfully (ଆପଣ / କରନ୍ତୁ forms). Jagannath-tradition devotional words
(ମହାପ୍ରସାଦ, ଶ୍ରୀବିଗ୍ରହ, ଭୋଗ) are natural. Mobile first: buttons, menu items,
labels and headings use the shortest natural wording.

## Never translate / keep as is
- Amounts and numbers: `₹2,000`, `80G`, `PAN`, phone numbers, dates in digits.
- UPI ID, email, URLs, `{placeholders}`.
- PhonePe, Google Pay, GPay, Paytm, BHIM, WhatsApp, Razorpay, UPI.
