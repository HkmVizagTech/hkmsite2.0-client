# Gujarati glossary — harekrishnavizag.org

Style: warm, respectful, simple everyday Gujarati (not heavy literary).
Address the reader respectfully with આપ (આપનું / આપના). Keep buttons, menus,
labels and badges as short as possible — the site is mostly read on phones.

## Never translate / keep as is
- Amounts and numbers: `₹2,000`, `Rs. 100`, `80G`, `PAN`, phone numbers, times (`4:30 AM`).
- UPI ID, email, URLs, `{placeholders}`.
- "PhonePe", "Google Pay", "GPay", "Paytm", "WhatsApp", "Razorpay", "UPI", "YouTube", "Google Maps", "Matchless Gifts".
