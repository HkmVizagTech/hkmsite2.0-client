# Tamil glossary — harekrishnavizag.org

Use these consistently. Style: warm, respectful, simple spoken-written Tamil
(not heavy literary Tamil). Address the reader respectfully (நீங்கள் /
-ுங்கள் forms). Mobile first: keep buttons, menu items, labels and headings as
short as possible.

## Never translate / keep as is
- Amounts and numbers: `₹2,000`, `80G`, `PAN`, phone numbers, dates in digits.
- Placeholders like `{amount}`, `{name}`.
- UPI ID, email, URLs, brand names: PhonePe, Google Pay, GPay, Paytm, BHIM, WhatsApp, Razorpay, UPI.
