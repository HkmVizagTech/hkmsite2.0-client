# Telugu glossary — harekrishnavizag.org

Use these consistently. Style: warm, respectful, simple spoken Telugu that a
Visakhapatnam family reads easily — not heavy literary Telugu. Address the
reader respectfully (మీరు / చేయండి forms).

## Never translate / keep as is
- Amounts and numbers: `₹2,000`, `80G`, `PAN`, phone numbers, dates in digits.
- UPI ID, email, URLs, brand logos.
- "PhonePe", "Google Pay", "GPay", "Paytm", "BHIM", "WhatsApp", "Razorpay", "UPI".
