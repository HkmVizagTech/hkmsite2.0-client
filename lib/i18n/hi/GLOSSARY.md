# Hindi glossary — harekrishnavizag.org

Style: warm, respectful, simple everyday Hindi (not heavy Sanskritised).
Address the reader as आप (करें / दें / देखें). Keep buttons, menu items,
labels and badges as short as the English (mobile 320–430px).

## Never translate / keep as is
- Amounts and numbers: `₹2,000`, `Rs. 100`, `80G`, `PAN`, `IFSC`, `NEFT / RTGS`, `sq ft`, phone numbers.
- Placeholders: `{amount}`, `{name}`, `{n}`, `{date}` etc.
- UPI ID, email, URLs. "PhonePe", "Google Pay", "GPay", "Paytm", "WhatsApp", "Razorpay", "UPI", "YouTube", "Google Maps", "Matchless Gifts".
